# Before launch

- [ ] Review the unpublished Shopify preview; do not publish the development build as-is.
- [ ] Confirm ownership, brand clearance, product naming and approved brand/About copy.
- [ ] Supply a transparent, high-resolution version of the provided offset symbol; the current asset is a small screenshot and preserves its proportions.
- [ ] Approve the campaign photograph and rights; supply an optional mobile-specific crop or photograph. Inspect both models and garments at desktop, tablet and mobile widths.
- [ ] Review all 108 AI-generated sample photographs against the manufactured garments; upload the matching media and replace any inaccurate images with verified photography. Add optional on-model photographs only when they show the correct garment and color.
- [ ] Verify every product's fabric, construction, fit, care, weight, price, currency and tax configuration. Imported information is demonstration data.
- [ ] Measure every garment and replace sample size tables. Confirm cm and flat-garment measurement conventions; set `sample` false only after verification.
- [ ] Replace all sample inventory, unique SKUs and fulfillment/location information with approved operational data.
- [ ] Activate only intended products and sales channels. Confirm each product has all nine genuine Shopify variants.
- [ ] Create automated collections, menus and About/contact pages; select the editable homepage collection and editorial links.
- [ ] Add merchant social profile URLs, support email/contact details and business information.
- [ ] Provide approved privacy, returns/refunds, shipping and terms policies in Shopify Settings. Unset policy names remain visibly marked as requiring merchant content.
- [ ] Configure Shopify's native filters and check every filter combination and reset action.
- [ ] Configure payment providers, shipping zones/rates, taxes, markets, currency and domain in Shopify.
- [ ] Test cart add, sold-out errors, stock limits, quantity updates, removal and empty states on the unpublished Shopify theme.
- [ ] Test Shopify hosted checkout navigation and a development/test order. Confirm delivery, tax, payment and confirmation behavior.
- [ ] Run keyboard/screen-reader and mobile-device checks with the final media and copy. Check slow connections and failed cart requests.
- [ ] Remove the development announcement, footer development note, `sample-data` tags and sample-product wording after replacing and approving all sample content.

No production deployment, product import, inventory modification or Shopify checkout testing was performed as part of this local build.
