# SIDEFORM — brand direction

**Brand idea:** Understated streetwear for people building their own path.

**Tagline:** Made for your own direction.

**Personality:** Quiet confidence, independent thinking, everyday creativity.

## Visual identity

- Wordmark: bold, closely spaced uppercase sans serif.
- Symbol: two parallel slanted bars, vertically offset. Use as a small chest mark, label detail, and packaging signature.
- Bone: `#F4F2EC` — primary background.
- Washed black: `#292C25` — typography and contrast.
- Muted olive: `#737A67` — supporting color.
- Typography: clean sans serif for functional text; restrained italic serif for editorial emphasis.
- Photography: natural light, concrete architecture, candid movement, relaxed silhouettes.

## Collection 001 — OFF THE GRID

Concept pieces: Offset Tee in washed black and bone, plus Form Long Sleeve in muted olive. Product images are AI-generated visual concepts; materials, measurements, pricing, sourcing, and availability remain undecided.

## Voice

Short, direct, self-assured language. Focus on form, personal expression, and everyday wear. Avoid invented quality, sustainability, origin, and scarcity claims.

## Homepage direction

Campaign photograph → concise introduction → three-piece product edit → brand perspective → collection invitation.

## Name status

SIDEFORM is a working name approved for concept development. Existing unrelated uses were found; trademark, business registration, domain, and social-handle availability have not been verified.
